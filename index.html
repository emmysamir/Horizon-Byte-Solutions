FAQ

How long until I hear back?

We reply by email within 48 hours of your application.

Can I apply to more than one role?

Yes, submit a separate application for each role. Applying twice for the same role isn't needed, and we'll let you know if we already have it.

Is AI used to review applications?

AI tools help summarize CVs and compare them with the role requirements. Our team reviews the results and makes the decisions.

I didn't get a confirmation. What now?

Check your spam folder first. If nothing arrives within 48 hours, apply again and we'll tell you whether we already have your application.
